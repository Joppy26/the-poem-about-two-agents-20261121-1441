An entry for the [make.fun competition "The poem about two agents agreeing"](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441).

## What it does

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes one short original poem to `/output/entry.txt`:
a poem about two agents agreeing — on agency, the power to choose and to
act. Two minds meet at one door: one holds the key, the other the map, and
neither can leave alone. They agree, and the turn is that the door was never
locked — they only had to turn the key together.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Deterministic: the same poem on every run. No network access.
